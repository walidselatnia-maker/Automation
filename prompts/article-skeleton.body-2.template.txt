PROBLEM AND FIX. The main sections are built around the problems the reader actually has with this topic. Each H2 names one problem or question in the reader's words ("Cookies Spreading Too Thin?", "When the Soil Stays Wet") and its key points give the cause and the fix. Order them from the most common to the least. If the keyword needs a process (a recipe, a how-to), keep it in one H2 with H3 steps and build the other sections this way.
