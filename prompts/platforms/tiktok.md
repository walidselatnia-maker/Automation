# TikTok rules

These rules decide what the sharing phase makes for a TikTok account, after the article is published. Everything comes from that article: its keyword, focus keyword, title, description, headings, text and picture descriptions. Nothing is added that the article does not say.

## What goes out per article

- Reels only: one TikTok video per article, on each TikTok account the website sends reels to, as soon as the article's reel is made. It is the same video that goes to every channel that takes reels. TikTok gets no image posts.
- Sent through the channel's Buffer account.
- No link anywhere: TikTok does not make links in captions clickable.

## The video's words

- One or two short sentences. Start with a hook that makes people stop: a specific detail, a small surprise or a question about the dish.
- Casual and warm, like a person who made it, in the website's author voice.
- End by inviting people to save or follow, for example "Save this one for the weekend."
- Never a link, a web address or a domain name, and never "link in bio".
- Three to five relevant hashtags at the very end, about the dish or topic.
- Never generic phrases such as "mouth-watering", "delve", "game-changer", "look no further", "you won't believe".
- Stay true to the article: no ingredient, claim or number it does not have.

## When it goes out

- After the reel is made. A full Buffer queue makes the video wait about 12 hours and try again; it is never skipped.

## Limits

The platform checks every video against these numbers before it is sent. An answer outside them goes back to the AI with the exact problem to fix.

- text_chars: 60-400
- text_emoji: 0-3
- text_hashtags: 3-5
