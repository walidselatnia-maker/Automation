# YouTube rules

These rules decide what the sharing phase makes for a YouTube channel, after the article is published. Everything comes from that article: its keyword, focus keyword, title, description, headings, text and picture descriptions. Nothing is added that the article does not say.

## What goes out per article

- Reels only: one YouTube Short per article, on each YouTube channel the website sends reels to, as soon as the article's reel is made. It is the same video that goes to every channel that takes reels. YouTube gets no image posts.
- Sent through the channel's Buffer account, in the How-to & Style category.
- No link anywhere: a link in a Short's words cannot be clicked.

## The Short's words

- Title: what the video shows, with the dish or topic's name near the start, the way people search for it. Plain words, no clickbait, no emoji, no hashtags.
- Text (the description): one or two short sentences about the dish, in the website's author voice, then two or three hashtags, the first one #shorts.
- Never a link, a web address or a domain name, and never "link in bio".
- Never generic phrases such as "mouth-watering", "delve", "game-changer", "look no further", "you won't believe".
- Stay true to the article: no ingredient, claim or number it does not have.

## When it goes out

- After the reel is made. A full Buffer queue makes the Short wait about 12 hours and try again; it is never skipped.

## Limits

The platform checks every Short against these numbers before it is sent. An answer outside them goes back to the AI with the exact problem to fix.

- title_chars: 20-90
- title_emoji: 0-0
- title_hashtags: 0-0
- text_chars: 60-400
- text_emoji: 0-2
- text_hashtags: 2-3
