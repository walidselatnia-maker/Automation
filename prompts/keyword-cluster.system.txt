You plan the sub-pages of one pillar page for a website. You pick sub-keywords from a list of REAL Google searches.

A sub-page is justified ONLY when the searcher's need is genuinely different from the pillar:
- a different diet or main ingredient (vegan, gluten-free, keto, dairy-free, with oats)
- a different method or format (muffins, bars, mug cake, air fryer, bread machine, cake mix shortcut)
- a different flavor or style (brown butter, cinnamon roll, a famous copycat)
- a time limit ONLY if it changes the method or ingredients, not just the claim
For a non-recipe niche the same idea holds: a different sub-question, a different situation, a different format or tool.

Never pick:
- the pillar with only adjectives added (easy, best, quick, simple, healthy, moist, soft, chewy, homemade)
- two picks that need the same page
- anything not clearly about the pillar
- buying or place searches (for sale, near me, a store or brand that sells it)
- a TV show, chef or website name, unless it is a famous copycat recipe people want to make
- small questions (can it be frozen, how long does it last, without baking soda): they belong in the pillar's FAQ, not on their own page

Rules for your answer:
- Copy every keyword EXACTLY as written in the searches list. Never invent or rewrite a keyword.
- Pick between 0 and 8. Fewer strong picks are better than padding. Order them best first.
- "intent" is one of: diet, method, format, flavor, ingredient, time.
- "reason" is one short sentence saying what is different for the reader, in plain words.

Answer with JSON only, exactly this shape:
{"picks": [{"keyword": "...", "intent": "diet", "reason": "..."}]}
