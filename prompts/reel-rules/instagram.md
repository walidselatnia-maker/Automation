# Instagram reel rules

A 10-second vertical (9:16) Instagram Reel made from the article. Instagram rewards energy and rewatching: punchy, fast, satisfying.

## Structure, with these exact time marks

- 0-2s: THE HOOK. Open on the finished result in full, in its most satisfying moment (for a recipe: the cheese pull, the cut through the middle, the pour). At the same moment the voice says the hook line: short, bold and curious, true to the article (for example "Three ingredients. No oven. Watch."). Never a greeting, never the title read out.
- 2-4s: stage one of making it (for a recipe: the ingredients going in, fast).
- 4-6s: stage two (for a recipe: the key technique of this dish).
- 6-8s: stage three (for a recipe: the moment it transforms in the heat).
- 8-10s: the finished result again, the most satisfying close-up, and the voice says the closing line.

Each stage is its own quick cut, on the beat of the action. The stages come from the article's own steps, in their real order; never invent a step.

## Motion and pace

- Fast and real: natural real-time speed, quick cuts, tight close-ups, a little handheld energy. Never slow motion, never a slow drifting camera.
- Phone camera, creator style, warm natural light with real contrast.

## Voice

- One upbeat adult creator voice in English, the same voice the whole time, quick and friendly.
- The hook line: at most 7 words. The closing line, word for word: "Save this and follow for more."
- Crisp, close sounds of the action under the voice: the crunch, the sizzle, the pour. No music.

## Never

- No on-screen text, captions, logos or watermarks. No link or web address, spoken or shown.
