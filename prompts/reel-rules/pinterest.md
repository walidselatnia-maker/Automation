# Pinterest reel rules

A 10-second vertical (9:16) Pinterest video pin made from the article. Pinterest viewers are planning: they save ideas that look beautiful and easy to make.

## Structure, with these exact time marks

- 0-2s: THE HOOK. Show the finished result in full, beautifully styled, filling the frame (for a recipe: the finished dish on a bright table). At the same moment the voice says the hook line: why this is worth saving, true to the article (for example "The easiest dessert you will make all summer"). Never a greeting, never the title read out.
- 2-4s: stage one of making it (for a recipe: the ingredients laid out or going into the bowl).
- 4-6s: stage two (for a recipe: the key step that makes this dish).
- 6-8s: stage three (for a recipe: the final assembly or the moment it comes out of the oven).
- 8-10s: the finished result again, a final touch (a garnish, a sprinkle, a slice), and the voice says the closing line.

Each stage is its own clean cut. The stages come from the article's own steps, in their real order; never invent a step.

## Motion and pace

- Natural real-time speed, smooth and steady hands, bright and tidy. Never slow motion, never floating objects.
- Bright soft daylight, light wood or white marble, clean styling, overhead and 45-degree angles.

## Voice

- One calm, warm adult voice in English, the same voice the whole time.
- The hook line: at most 10 words. The closing line, word for word: "Save this for later."
- Soft natural sounds of the action under the voice. No music.

## Never

- No on-screen text, captions, logos or watermarks. No link or web address, spoken or shown.
