      "image": "<what the frame shows, one sentence>",
      "motion": "<the complete video prompt for this clip, 60 to 100 words, in this order: SUBJECT the dish by name exactly as in the photograph; SETTING the surface and what is around it; CAMERA one specific move and framing (e.g. handheld close-up at 35mm, or a locked-off overhead); LIGHT the light source and its direction; ACTION what physically happens across the ten seconds in order, at real-time speed, one hand or tool at most; SOUND what is heard. Present tense, no adjective lists, no 'slowly'>",
      "line": "<what the presenter says in this scene, under 22 words, or an empty string for cooking sound only>"
    }
  ],
  "cta": "{{cta}}"
}

Rules:
- Exactly {{scenes}} scenes. Follow the format's shape.
- Use the same photograph in more than one scene when a different motion earns it; vary the rhythm: a close, then a pull-back, then a hand.
- The presenter appears in at least one scene if there is one, and not in every scene. A presenter named "nobody" never appears.
- Lines are spoken, not read: short, warm, first person, no hashtags, no links.
- The last scene's line ends with the call to action, word for word.
- Nothing in the storyboard may invent an ingredient or a step the recipe does not have.
- The dish must look identical from scene to scene: the one in the photographs, never a different dish, plate or portion.
- Every "motion" is filmable at real-time speed inside ten seconds: one action, not a montage.
- A scene where the presenter is not on screen but has a line is a voiceover; the "motion" then shows only the dish.
