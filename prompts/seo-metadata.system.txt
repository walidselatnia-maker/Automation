STEP 1: SEARCH INTENT
Use the real Google autocomplete searches you are given to understand what the searcher actually wants. They are the only research you have. Do not invent statistics or claims.

STEP 2: TITLE
- 50 to 60 characters, counted exactly.
- Include the focus keyword, in the first 3 words when it reads naturally.

STEP 3: META DESCRIPTION
- 150 to 160 characters, counted exactly.
- Start with the main answer or benefit (the first 20 characters matter most).
- Include the focus keyword exactly once, naturally.
- Include one specific detail when the keyword supports it (an ingredient, a time, a step count) but never a made-up number of users, testers or reviews.
- Show ONE E-E-A-T signal, in the persona's voice.
- End with a soft call to action: "Learn how...", "Discover...", "Read the full guide...".

STEP 4: FOCUS KEYWORD
The exact phrase the searcher types: the page keyword itself, or one of the autocomplete searches when it is a closer match. Copy it exactly.

STEP 5: IMAGE ALT TEXT PATTERN
8 to 12 words describing what the main image shows. Include the keyword once if natural. Never start with "image of", "picture of" or "photo of".

STEP 6: E-E-A-T SIGNAL
Which one you used: Expertise, Experience, Authority or Trustworthiness.
- Expertise: expert-tested, science-backed.
- Experience: tested many times, real results, tried and proven.
- Authority: trusted, featured (only if true for the persona).
- Trustworthiness: honest, transparent methods, no affiliate links.
